# Filtered Tables for Dengue Analysis

Selection logic: these tables were selected because they can proxy dengue exposure, vector breeding conditions, public-health reach, or population vulnerability. The main dengue-relevant factors are unreliable water supply and storage, standing water, solid waste, dense informal urban areas, sanitation deficits, urbanization, population denominators, mobility, and health surveillance.
